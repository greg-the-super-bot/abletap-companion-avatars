  • Automatically: our hosting provider (Netlify) and product analytics (PostHog, on the homepage and the early-access page) collect standard technical information — browser and device type, referring page, and page-view events — to keep the site secure and understand how it performs. How we use information

  • To add you to the launch waitlist and notify you when AbleTap opens

  • To operate, secure, and improve the AbleTap.io website
  • To count advertising visits, only if you accept and only when an advertising ID is configured

Cookies and advertising measurement

Two kinds of measurement can run on this website.

  • PostHog loads on the homepage and on /early-access/ for page views. It does not wait for the advertising-cookie banner. After a waitlist signup returns a successful response, the site can send one PostHog event, waitlist_signup, with utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, utm_term, and the landing path. The email address you typed is not a property of that event. TODO: confirm PostHog retention and the storage keys it sets.
  • Meta Pixel and the LinkedIn Insight Tag load only after you choose Accept, and only when the matching ID is set in the site config. With the IDs empty, Accept does not load either tag. The Meta tag sends a PageView only. We do not send custom events, and we do not send health, disability, or diagnosis information. The LinkedIn tag counts the page visit. We do not add a tag for browsers that have JavaScript turned off, so declining or blocking scripts keeps them off.

If you choose Decline, we store that choice in local storage on your device under the key abletap_consent and we do not load the Meta or LinkedIn tags. Accept is stored the same way. Use Cookie choices in the footer to change it. Clearing site data for abletap.io removes the choice.

When those tags load, they receive the page URL, the referring URL, and technical browser data those companies use to count a visit, and they may set their own cookies. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export the personal information we hold about you, or to opt out of communications. To make a request, email info@abletap.io and we'll respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You can unsubscribe from waitlist emails at any time. You can decline advertising tags, or change that choice, with Cookie choices in the footer.

Children's privacy

The AbleTap.io website and waitlist are intended for adults — parents, caregivers, family members, and professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from children through this website. AbleTap is designed to support people with disabilities, some of whom may be minors; when the app launches we will provide age-appropriate protections and caregiver controls, described in the product's Privacy Policy. If you believe a child has provided us information through this website, email info@abletap.io and we will delete it promptly.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit (TLS). No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

AbleTap.io is operated from the United States. If you visit from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.
