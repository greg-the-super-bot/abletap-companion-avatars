Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

AbleTap ("AbleTap," "we," "us," or "our") is a product of Chill, LLC, an Arkansas limited liability company. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit AbleTap.io and join our launch waitlist.

AbleTap is coming soon. This policy covers the current AbleTap.io website and waitlist. When the AbleTap app launches, a fuller product Privacy Policy will describe the information the app itself handles (accounts, communication and AAC content, and device usage) and the protections around it. AbleTap is also a product of Chill, LLC — see Chill's privacy policy.

Information we collect

  • Waitlist sign-up: when you join the launch waitlist, you provide your email address so we can notify you when AbleTap is ready.
  • If you email us at info@abletap.io, we receive your email address and message so we can reply.
  • Automatically: our hosting provider and privacy-first analytics collect standard technical information — browser and device type, referring page, and aggregated page-view events — to keep the site secure and understand how it performs. We do not use advertising trackers.

How we use information

  • To add you to the launch waitlist and notify you when AbleTap opens
  • To respond to messages you send us
  • To operate, secure, and improve the AbleTap.io website
  • To comply with legal obligations

Service providers

To run this website we rely on a small number of processors, currently Netlify (website hosting and waitlist form handling) and PostHog (privacy-first product analytics). We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising or marketing.

How long we keep information

We keep your waitlist email until launch and a reasonable period afterward, and email correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request. You can ask us to remove you from the waitlist or delete your correspondence at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export the personal information we hold about you, or to opt out of communications. To make a request, email info@abletap.io and we'll respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You can unsubscribe from waitlist emails at any time.

Children's privacy

The AbleTap.io website and waitlist are intended for adults — parents, caregivers, family members, and professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from children through this website. AbleTap is designed to support people with disabilities, some of whom may be minors; when the app launches we will provide age-appropriate protections and caregiver controls, described in the product's Privacy Policy. If you believe a child has provided us information through this website, email info@abletap.io and we will delete it promptly.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit (TLS). No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International visitors

AbleTap.io is operated from the United States. If you visit from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, write to info@abletap.io.
