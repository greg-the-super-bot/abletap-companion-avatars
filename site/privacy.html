Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026 · AbleTap is a product of Chill LLC, an Arkansas limited liability company.

AbleTap.io is currently a pre-launch informational site. This policy explains what little we collect here, and how we handle it. When the AbleTap app launches, this policy will be expanded to cover the app itself before you can use it.

What we collect on this site

  • Waitlist email. If you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you enter. Submissions are processed by Netlify Forms and a notification is delivered to our team inbox. We use your email only to tell you about AbleTap's launch and early access. No marketing lists are sold or shared.
  • Basic analytics. We use PostHog to understand how visitors use this site (pages viewed, buttons clicked). IP addresses are anonymized, and anonymous visitors are not identified by name or email. We do not use session replay on this site.
  • Companion voice previews. The "talk to" buttons play pre-recorded audio clips. They do not use your microphone, and no conversation happens or is recorded on this site.

What we don't collect

No accounts, no payments, no location, no contacts, no microphone or camera access, no health information. If a child visits this page, we knowingly collect nothing from them; the waitlist is intended for adults.

Service providers

  • Netlify — website hosting and waitlist form processing.
  • PostHog — product analytics (IPs anonymized).
  • Google Fonts — typefaces served by Google.

Your choices

Want your waitlist email removed? Write to info@abletap.io and we'll delete it promptly. You can also ask what information we hold about you — for this site, it will only ever be your waitlist email and anonymous usage statistics.

Changes

We'll update the date at the top when this policy changes. A full policy covering the AbleTap app — including its accessibility features, voice interactions, and data handling — will be published before launch, because families deserve to know exactly how a tool like this works before they trust it.
