Our Mission

Technology should work for everyone

We're parents. We know the feeling of watching your loved one try to turn on a show or open an app and wishing — more than anything — that you could just make it easier for them. AbleTap.io is us doing exactly that.

Our Daughter Inspired This

AbleTap.io started with our daughter — a nonverbal young woman with special needs who deserved to pick her own shows, use her own devices, and live with real independence. We couldn't find the right tool for her, so we built it. She was in our hearts through every single decision.

AI That Actually Gets It

Think of Ava like that one teacher who just clicks with your person — endlessly patient, never rushed, never frustrated. She uses pictures, familiar words, and gentle encouragement. No tech jargon. No overwhelm. Just calm, steady support.

Every Win Matters

The first time they turn on their own show. The first time they call you without help. Those moments? They're everything. AbleTap.io grows alongside your loved one — stepping back when they're ready, stepping in when they need a hand.

What AbleTap.io Does

One AI friend. By their side.

Whether it's picking tonight's show, calling a family member, or finding the right words — Ava is right there, calm and friendly, making it feel like something they can totally do.

TV & Streaming Control

Tap the picture of the show — and it plays. No menus to dig through, no remote to fumble with. Netflix, Disney+, YouTube, Hulu — all just one picture away. This was the first thing we built, and it changed everything for our daughter.

Works with Roku · Fire TV coming

Phone & Text

Tap a contact and call the people they love. During the call, the AAC board flips up so they can talk through it in their own words — and Ava can help fill the quiet. No rushing, no judgment, ever. Plus simple text to stay close between calls.

Phone calls · Text · Coming soon

AAC & Communication

Real AAC, built from lived family experience. If your loved one has ever used TouchChat, Proloquo2Go, or a picture board, AbleTap.io will feel familiar from the very first tap — the same visual language they already know. A gentle prediction row suggests the next word they choose — they stay the author, always.

Familiar from the first tap · Works on its own

Caregiver Dashboard

You're already doing so much. Setup should take minutes, not days. Add their favorite shows and the people they love to call. Create profiles for each person in the house. Then step back and let them shine.

Easy Setup · iCloud Sync
